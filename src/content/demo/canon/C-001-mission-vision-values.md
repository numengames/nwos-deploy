# Mission

Faro Austral keeps the machinery of small fishing and research vessels
running, so that crews come home and the work at sea is not interrupted by
what could have been foreseen on land.

# Vision

To be the workshop the fleets of the Rías Baixas call first — the one that
is trusted with the repair nobody else wants to sign off, and the one whose
reports the shipowners read because they are true.

# Values

**Say what the vessel needs.** A diagnosis is not a sales argument. When a
repair is not worth doing, we say so and we say what we would do instead.

**The sea sets the deadline, not us.** A tide, a season and a weather
window are not negotiable. We plan around them and we commit to dates we
can keep.

**The person who did the work signs it.** Every intervention carries the
name of whoever did it. It is how we learn, not how we blame.

**Teach while you work.** Nobody holds a procedure in their head alone. If
only one person can do it, it is not yet a capability of the cooperative.

**One member, one voice.** The cooperative decides by the people who work
in it, not by who brought the most capital.
