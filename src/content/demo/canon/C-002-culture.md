# Culture principles

**The workshop before the office.** Decisions about a repair are taken where
the repair happens, with the people doing it.

**Written down beats remembered.** A procedure that lives only in a head
leaves with that head. Anything done twice gets written once.

**Mistakes are reported the same day.** A failure found early costs a part;
found late, it costs a vessel and a reputation.

# How we work

The cooperative works in **weeks**: on Monday morning the work of the week
is agreed in front of the board — vessel, job, who takes it, which tide it
has to be ready for. On Friday, whatever closed is written down in two
lines: what was done, what was learned.

Shore work and sea work are different trades, and nobody is asked to do
both on the same day.

Quotations go out within 48 hours or the client is told why not.

# Communication style

Plain Galician and Spanish with clients, written Spanish in documents.
Short messages, no jargon with shipowners, exact part names with suppliers.
Bad news travels first and whole.
