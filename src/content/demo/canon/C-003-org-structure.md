# Leadership team

The cooperative is governed by its **General Assembly**: every working
member, one vote each, meeting four times a year.

Between assemblies it is run by a **Governing Council** of three members
elected for four years, and by a **managing director** appointed by the
Council.

# Departments / areas

| Workshop — hull and welding | Steel and aluminium repairs, dry-dock work   |

| Field service               | Interventions on board, in port and at sea   |
| Quotations and client care  | Diagnosis, estimates, follow-up              |
| Administration              | Invoicing, purchasing, cooperative paperwork |

# Key roles

- **Area lead** — plans the week of their area and signs off its work.
- **Field technician** — takes a job from diagnosis to the client's
  signature.
- **Apprentice** — learns beside a technician; every procedure done twice
  is written down.
- **Safety officer** — required by law for work on board; stops any job.
