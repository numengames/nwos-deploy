# Glossary

The words this organisation uses, so that a new member, a client or an AI
agent reads the same meaning.

| Term                         | Definition                                                 | Context                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Tide window                  | The hours in which a vessel can enter or leave the yard    | Every dry-dock job is planned around one           |
| Dry dock                     | Taking the vessel out of the water for hull work           | Booked weeks ahead; losing a slot costs the season |
| Sea trial                    | Running the vessel after a repair to prove it              | Nothing is invoiced before it passes               |
| Work order                   | The document that opens a job: vessel, fault, who takes it | The unit of work of the cooperative                |
| Diagnosis                    | What is actually wrong, written before any estimate        | Separated from the estimate on purpose             |
| Estimate                     | Price and dates offered to the shipowner                   | Valid 30 days                                      |
| Field intervention           | Work done on board instead of in the workshop              | Charged by displacement plus hours                 |
| Shipowner                    | Whoever owns the vessel and pays the work                  | Not always the skipper                             |
| Skipper                      | Whoever commands the vessel at sea                         | The person the technician talks to                 |
| Fleet                        | A set of vessels under one shipowner                       | Framework agreements are signed per fleet          |
| Classification society       | The body that certifies a vessel's condition               | Some repairs need its inspector present            |
| Certificate of seaworthiness | The paper without which the vessel does not sail           | Repairs are scheduled against its expiry           |
| Working member               | A cooperative member who works in it and votes             | One member, one vote                               |
| General Assembly             | The meeting where the members decide                       | Four times a year                                  |
| Cooperative return           | The share of the surplus returned to members               | Agreed at the closing assembly                     |
| Preventive maintenance       | Planned work to avoid a breakdown                          | Sold as a yearly plan                              |
| Corrective maintenance       | Work after a breakdown                                     | What the emergency line handles                    |
| Spare part lead time         | Days from ordering a part to having it                     | The main cause of a missed date                    |
| Hot work                     | Welding or cutting on board                                | Needs a permit and a fire watch                    |
| Lay-up                       | A vessel stopped for a season                              | The time to do everything postponed                |
